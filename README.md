SOLMAR CITY is an original open-world crime game: Marco Reyes owes $50,000 to loan shark Vera Kross and must finish six story missions across Port Solano — stealing cars, courier runs, gang shakedowns, police getaways, drops, and a final payday. Spend mission money at Dex's garage on Engine, Armor and Tires upgrades that really change handling and durability, buy a pistol, SMG or rifle at the weapon shop, and take repeatable side jobs — taxi fares, package deliveries and stunt jumps — while a living city of 20 traffic cars, 32 pedestrians, foot and motorcycle police, a wanted system, a day/night cycle and procedural audio reacts to everything you do.
